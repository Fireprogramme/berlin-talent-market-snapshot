05 / RESEARCH NOTES

Methodology & limitations

01

Purpose

Demonstrate how public vacancy records can be structured into actionable, auditable recruitment research.

02

Collection

Seven previously recorded advertisements from employer-linked career pages. Original links are retained. Source content and availability have not yet been reverified.

03

Cleaning

Each skill counts at most once per advertisement. Equivalent labels such as “Spark” and “Apache Spark” are normalized; PySpark remains distinct.

04

Interpretation

Seniority and language categories are derived from stored descriptions and may require correction during manual source review.

05

Verification

Records are unverified by default. Change a record to active or expired only after opening the original source and documenting the result.

06

Limitations

Small, non-random, data-role-heavy sample; not a live feed, market-wide vacancy count, hiring trend or representative estimate.

Research roadmap

Audit each original listing, record a genuine verification date, separate required from preferred skills, expand across junior/mid/senior roles, and only then publish stronger market conclusions.
